## Full-text correction audit

- C01 was re-read at §4–§6: both cases are in Naples and surroundings; the air-taxi case is Capodichino airport to Naples business centre/port, and the medical case is Monte di Procida to Procida island. The method is BIT*/OMPL for strategic planning and three tactical levels (velocity scaling, local RRT, global RRT). Unsupported Rome–Naples wording was removed.
- C05 Table III/IV and §VI were rechecked. The text now states only the supported comparison that DCB+MARL has lower ground delay than DCB+rule at the three matched demand levels; it does not compare that delay to the no-intervention row.
- C07 was re-read at §2 and §4.4: 15 aircraft is the current walkthrough count; 15 aircraft/hour and 30% workload are related DLR simulation results. The current walkthrough does not provide the C08 trajectory-tool separation parameters, so those values were removed from C07.
- C08 was re-read at §2.2.8 and §3: the framework defines slot-based and trajectory-based approaches, but the reported 4-hour Hamburg study applies slot-based management; the JSON was corrected accordingly.
- C09, C10, and C11 numerical values and author-stated future/limitation passages were rechecked against their listed sections and tables; no unsupported cross-paper values are retained in those fields.
