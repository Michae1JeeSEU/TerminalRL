The new candidate6s limit counts cumulative **active candidate work**, including candidate generation and attributable single-column validation; selection time is excluded. Targets sum actually measured generator, energy and attributable validation work; missing components and ambiguous contribution counterfactuals remain masked. CP wall time is never allocated to individual candidates. The two bounded reference callbacks can reveal missed better solutions in their observed catalogs; they cannot exhaustively detect quality loss.

The ReplayBundle retains authoritative world states, events and committed plans; aggregate online counters replace raw feature arrays. No quality degradation tolerance is authorized.
